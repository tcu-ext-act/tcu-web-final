Al Seeb awards main contract for Chedi Private Residences

With a total built-up area of around 1.25m sqft, the skyscraper will rise about 250m across 53-storeys featuring 117-residences

Author: TCU Admin
XIN🔗

With a total built-up area of around 1.25m sqft, the skyscraper will rise about 250m across 53-storeys featuring 117-residences


Al Seeb Real Estate Development has awarded a key contract to Ancient Builders Constructions (ABC) for the construction of The Chedi Private Residences, a US $200mn tower on Sheikh Zayed Road in Dubai. The agreement was signed by Mahesh Kalwani, the Vice Chairman of Al Seeb, and Zeeshan Ali Akhtar, the Managing Director of ABC in Dubai.


With a total built-up area of around 1.25m sqft, the skyscraper will rise about 250m across 53-storeys and will feature 117-residences with a mix of villas, duplexes and mansions, said Al Seeb in a statement.
