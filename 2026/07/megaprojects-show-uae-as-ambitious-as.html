Megaprojects show UAE as ambitious as ever despite Iran attacks

Despite the Iran war, the UAE continues advancing mega projects, investing in transport infrastructure, entertainment, and long-term economic growth.

Author: TCU Admin
XIN🔗

Large-scale projects in the UAE, ranging from transport infrastructure to world-class entertainment venues, remain on track despite the Iran war.


The Emirates is not limiting its ambitions as it invests in its future and advances its global standing.


Joseph Salem, a partner at the consultancy Arthur D Little in Dubai, said the launch of schemes as work continues on others even after the regional conflict began this year showed that growth would continue.
