Top 12 project launches in June 2026: Emaar, Cheval Maison, Nobu and more

See the top 12 real estate projects launched in June across the UAE and Saudi Arabia, highlighting Emaar mega masterplan and other major development

TCU Admin
XIN🔗

June was another milestone month for the region’s property sector, with developers unveiling projects that reflect both the scale of regional ambition and the changing priorities of investors and residents. Across the UAE and Saudi Arabia, new launches placed greater emphasis on integrated communities, premium amenities, and future-ready urban planning. Scroll below to discover the top twelve project launches in June from Emaar, Nobu, RTA, and beyond. Please note that this list is not ranked.

Emaar – New masterplanx

Emaar Properties is set to unveil a landmark AED 200 bn masterplanned destination in the heart of Dubai, redefining urban living at an unprecedented scale. Spanning over 4.5 million square metres, the development will bring together luxury residences, Grade-A offices, retail, hospitality, and cultural spaces within a fully integrated “city within a city” designed for around 150,000 residents.
