About TheConstructionUpdate

Independent coverage, built for people who build things.

TheConstructionUpdate was founded by a small team of construction reporters and former field engineers who were tired of trade coverage written for anyone but the people actually running projects. We cover the news that affects your bid, your schedule and your crew — and we publish field-tested insight from people who've actually run a jobsite.

  • ✓ Independent, ad-supported newsroom with no paywall
  • ✓ Written and edited by former field engineers and project managers
  • ✓ Covering infrastructure, commercial, residential and heavy civil
  • ✓ New reporting published daily, every weekday
