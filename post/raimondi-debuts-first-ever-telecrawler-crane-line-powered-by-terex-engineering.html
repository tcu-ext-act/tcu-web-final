Raimondi Debuts First-Ever Telecrawler Crane Line, Powered By Terex Engineering

The new line debuts with the TTC 70, a telecrawler crane designed, engineered, and manufactured at the Group’s rough terrian headquarters in Italy

Author: TCU Admin
XIN🔗
Raimondi’s Terex Rough Terrain Cranes division re-enters the crawler segment with a new telecrawler crane line, marking a major strategic milestone for the Group

Building on the acquisition of Terex’s key lifting brands, Raimondi Group enhances its position as a multi-segment lifting solution provider with a bold return to a legacy product category.


Terex Rough Terrain Cranes, a Raimondi business division, is returning to the crawler segment with the launch of a new telecrawler crane line, marking a significant strategic milestone for the Group.


The new line debuts with the TTC 70, a telecrawler crane designed, engineered, and manufactured at the Group’s rough terrain headquarters in Crespellano, Bologna, Italy. The model lays the foundation for a renewed product category, with additional models planned for the near future.


“The unveiling of the TTC 70 marks a pivotal moment for Raimondi as we continue to evolve into a fully integrated, multi-segment lifting solutions provider, meeting the evolving needs of the global lifting industry,” said Luigi Maggioni, Chief Executive Officer, Raimondi Group.
