LAVA appointed Custodian of Expo 2030 Riyadh Master plan

LAVA appointed as Custodian of Expo 2030 Riyadh Master plan as the project transitions into detailed design and implementation stage

Author: TCU Admin
XIN🔗

LAVA (Laboratory for Visionary Architecture), a global network practice with offices in Germany and Australia, has been appointed as the Custodian of the Expo 2030 Riyadh Master plan. This appointment signifies a pivotal moment in the project’s journey, as it transitions into the detailed design and implementation phase.


Running from October 1, 2030, to March 31, 2031, Expo 2030 Riyadh will be one of the ambitious World Expos ever conceived. It will feature 6m sqm site that will host 197 nations, 29 organisations, and over 42m visitors across five distinct districts.


Expo 2030 Riyadh, held under the theme ‘Foresight for Tomorrow’ in the city of action and ambition, Riyadh.
