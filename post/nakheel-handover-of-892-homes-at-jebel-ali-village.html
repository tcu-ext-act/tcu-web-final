Nakheel commences handover of 892 homes at Jebel Ali Village

Nakheel begins handover of 892 homes at Jebel Ali Village, marking the beginning of residents’ move into this new residential community

Author: TCU Admin
XIN🔗

The destination pays homage to the area’s heritage while contributing to Jebel Ali’s ongoing transformation into a well-connected, family-oriented neighborhood


Nakheel, a member of Dubai Holding Real Estate, has commenced the handover of 892 homes at Jebel Ali Village, marking the beginning of residents’ move into this new residential community. The development is anticipated to accommodate 5,500 residents.


Jebel Ali Village is designed as a low-density residential community that integrates landscaped parks, pedestrian-friendly streets, outdoor recreation areas, and connected green spaces. These features are intended to foster community interaction and promote active living.


The destination pays homage to the area’s heritage while contributing to Jebel Ali’s ongoing transformation into a well-connected, family-oriented neighborhood.
