Red Sea Global opens 110-key Rosewood AMAALA Resort in Saudi Arabia

Rosewood AMAALA opens at Triple Bay, adding 110 keys, branded residences and a major wellness offering.

Author: TCU Admin
XIN🔗

Red Sea Global (RSG) has opened Rosewood AMAALA, a 110-key luxury resort at Triple Bay on Saudi Arabia’s northwestern Red Sea coast.


The opening marks another milestone in the phased development of AMAALA, which is being developed as a flagship wellness and luxury tourism destination in the Kingdom.


Located across approximately 40 hectares, the low-density resort has been designed by Antonio Citterio Patricia Viel (ACPV) to integrate with its natural surroundings.
